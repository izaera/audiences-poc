Our story

Northwind started in 2019 as a side project between two friends who were tired of bloated enterprise tools. Today we serve thousands of teams in more than 40 countries, but we still build every feature with the same question in mind: does this actually help?

We are a remote-first company with team members in Barcelona, Lisbon, Porto, and Berlin.

What we value

1

Clarity over cleverness

The obvious solution is usually the right one. We write code, docs, and emails the same way.

2

Ship small, ship often

Frequent, predictable releases beat occasional big bangs — for us and for our customers.

3

Respect the user's time

No dark patterns, no dead ends. If a feature does not pull its weight, we take it out.
